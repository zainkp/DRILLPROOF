# 01 — Product Brief

## Product
**Name:** DRILLPROOF  
**Category:** Emergency Readiness Management System  
**Tagline:** Prepared. Proven.

## The problem
Many institutions have emergency plans, equipment, contact lists, and assigned responsibilities on paper. But having a plan does not mean people know how to execute it.

Common operational gaps:
- Staff and occupants may not know their roles or evacuation routes.
- Safety equipment checks can be irregular or recorded in paper registers.
- Emergency contacts and building information can become outdated.
- Drills may happen infrequently or without consistent measurement.
- Issues discovered during drills (blocked exits, unclear instructions, missing supplies) may not be assigned, tracked, and verified to closure.
- Management often lacks one clear view of readiness across buildings, floors, teams, and open issues.

The core gap is **the distance between documented emergency plans and operational readiness**.

## The solution
DRILLPROOF is a digital platform for schools, colleges, hostels, offices, workplaces, hospitals, factories, and other multi-occupant facilities.

It helps an institution:
1. Digitize essential building emergency information.
2. Make that information quickly accessible through QR codes.
3. Record routine safety inspections.
4. Plan and run structured mock drills.
5. Record attendance/accountability and drill observations.
6. Convert observations into assigned corrective actions.
7. Review readiness through dashboards and reports.

The platform complements emergency services and qualified safety professionals. It does not replace them.

## Target customers
Initial beachhead:
- Private schools and colleges
- Hostels and campus facilities
- Offices and co-working spaces

Expansion segments:
- Hospitals and clinics
- Factories and warehouses
- Event venues and commercial buildings

Likely buyers/users:
- Institution owner or management
- Facility / administration manager
- EHS or safety officer
- HR / operations manager
- Building or floor wardens

## Hazard coverage
The product must not look like a fire-only application. Design the data model and interface around **incident types / hazards**, including:
- Fire and smoke
- Earthquake
- Flood / water ingress
- Severe weather
- Chemical or gas leak
- Medical emergency
- Electrical hazard
- Security-related evacuation

The first demo may fully configure only a few example drill templates, but the product language and structure should support multiple hazards.

## Value proposition
For institutions: one practical place to maintain emergency information, check readiness, run drills, and close safety gaps.

For management: visibility into what is prepared, what has been tested, and what still needs attention.

For staff: clearer responsibilities, accessible instructions, and structured drill participation.

## Business model hypothesis
A B2B subscription model, potentially tiered by site/building count and feature depth.

Illustrative pricing hypotheses only (not validated market rates):
- Starter: around ₹999/month — one small site, QR emergency page, basic checklists.
- Professional: around ₹2,999/month — multiple floors/buildings, drills, staff roles, reports.
- Enterprise: ₹7,999+/month — multiple sites, advanced administration, exports and custom workflows.

Possible additional revenue:
- One-time onboarding and emergency-plan digitization.
- Paid setup / training / drill facilitation through qualified partners.
- Enterprise annual contracts.

Do not present these prices or revenue projections as proven. Call them proposed pricing or assumptions until validated with customers.

## Positioning and differentiation
Do not claim that no emergency-management software exists. Existing safety, compliance, and emergency-management products may cover parts of this space.

The intended positioning is an accessible, operational readiness workflow for institutions:
- Plan information → accessible building guidance
- Inspection → issue → assigned corrective action → closure
- Drill → measured outcome → lessons learned → improvement
- Multi-building readiness visibility for management

The differentiation to test is simplicity, continuity of use, and connecting drills to corrective action—not a claim of being the first product in the category.

## What success looks like
A user can understand the product in under a minute and complete a convincing end-to-end workflow:
- Open a building's QR emergency page.
- Log in as an administrator.
- Review readiness status.
- Record an inspection or start a drill.
- Record drill progress and observations.
- Create and close a corrective action.
- See the dashboard/report update.
